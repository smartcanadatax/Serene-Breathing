Which App Is Right for You?

Choose Calm if...

You want the largest library of sleep stories, celebrity meditations (LeBron James, Matthew McConaughey), and music tracks. Calm has the most content of any meditation app. Worth it if you use it daily.

Choose Headspace if...

You prefer a structured, course-based approach to meditation. Headspace is great for beginners who want to learn meditation step by step with animated guides.

Choose Serene Breathing if...

You want to start with breathing exercises and meditation without committing $70/year upfront. The free tier includes real, usable features — box breathing, morning meditation, daily practice, and Need Calm Now — not just a 7-day trial.
