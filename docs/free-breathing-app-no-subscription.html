Why Most Breathing Apps Charge a Subscription

Apps like Calm and Headspace have large teams, celebrity partnerships, and expensive content production. Their business model requires ongoing subscription revenue to survive.

Serene Breathing is a small independent app. There are no celebrities, no massive content library, and no investor pressure to monetize every user. The core breathing and meditation features stay free permanently.

What Is the Premium Version?

Serene Breathing does have an optional premium upgrade that unlocks additional features — more ambient sound tracks, extended meditation sessions, and advanced AI coaching. But the core app — all breathing exercises, basic meditation, sleep sounds, journal, Apple Watch, and Apple Health — is completely free with no time limit.

🔒 Privacy First
